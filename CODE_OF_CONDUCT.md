# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Treat contributors and users with respect. Give specific technical feedback without attacking the person. Assume good intent, but protect maintainers from harassment, spam, and repeated disregard for written project boundaries.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- Explain problems with evidence.
- Accept a maintainer's scope decision.
- Keep public discussion professional.
- Protect private and security-sensitive information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, publishing private information, and sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [info@lupinum.com](mailto:info@lupinum.com). Lupinum OG may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

This policy applies in Lupinum repositories, documentation, community spaces, and direct project-related communication.
